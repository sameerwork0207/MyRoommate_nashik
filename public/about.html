Finding a place to stay in a new city shouldn't be the hardest part of college. Yet, students consistently face fake listings, exorbitant broker fees, and unverified, unsafe accommodations.

We started in Nashik, Maharashtra with a simple mission: to fix the broken student rental market. We realized that traditional real estate platforms aren't built for students. They lack transparency and cater to commercial dealers.

That's why we created MyRoommate. A curated, student-first platform where every PG, hostel, and flat is physically verified by our team, ensuring zero brokerage and 100% peace of mind for both students and parents.
