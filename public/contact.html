Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

How do I book a property?
You can schedule a free visit through our platform. Once you visit and like the property, our support team will guide you through the digital booking and documentation process directly with the property manager.
Are the listings actually verified?
Yes, 100%. Our internal operations team physically visits every PG, hostel, and flat before it is listed on MyRoommate. We verify amenities, safety, and hygiene standards to ensure zero surprises for students.
Is brokerage charged?
Absolutely not. MyRoommate is a zero-brokerage platform built for students. You only pay the actual rent and deposit directly to the property manager.
How do property visits work?
You select a date and time slot on the property page. Our system coordinates with the property manager, and you will receive a confirmation message with the exact location and contact details for the visit.
Can parents contact support?
Yes, we highly encourage parents to reach out. We understand the concerns of sending a child to a new city, and our team is happy to guide parents towards the safest and most reliable student accommodations.
